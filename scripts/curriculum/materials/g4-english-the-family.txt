Theme "The Family" (KICD Grade 4 English curriculum design). Like every English theme in this curriculum, it's taught across all four strands together — Listening & Speaking, Reading, Language Use (grammar), and Writing — using this theme's vocabulary and context throughout.

Suggested vocabulary: family, relative, care-giver, sibling, orphan, widow, clan, ancestor, mother-in-law, father-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, elder, old, young, adopt, twin, love, home, chore, duties, visitors, friend.

Cover: pronunciation and vocabulary practice using this theme's words; a reading passage in this theme's context with comprehension questions; a grammar/language-use point suited to Grade 4; and a short piece of writing on this theme.
