Sub-strand 1.1 Classification of Plants (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: classify plants into flowering and non-flowering; describe functions of parts of a flower; outline the importance of flowers in nature; appreciate the importance of flowers in nature.

Content covered: classification of plants (flowering and non-flowering plants); parts and functions of flowers.

Key inquiry question: How are plants classified?
