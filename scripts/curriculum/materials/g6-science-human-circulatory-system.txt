Sub-strand 1.3 Human circulatory system (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify main parts of the human circulatory system; describe functions of main parts of the human circulatory system; outline the symptoms and prevention of common health conditions of the human circulatory system; develop a routine plan for maintaining a healthy circulatory system; appreciate the human circulatory system.

Content covered: parts of the human circulatory system and their functions (heart — auricles, ventricles and vessels; blood vessels and blood); major blood vessels and their functions (arteries, veins and capillaries); components of blood and their functions (red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets).

Key inquiry questions: What is the human circulatory system made up of? What measures enhance a healthy human circulatory system?
