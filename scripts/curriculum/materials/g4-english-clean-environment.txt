Theme "Clean Environment" (KICD Grade 4 English curriculum design). Like every English theme in this curriculum, it's taught across all four strands together — Listening & Speaking, Reading, Language Use (grammar), and Writing — using this theme's vocabulary and context throughout.

Suggested vocabulary: compound, bin, bush, tree, conserve, water, clear, care, sweep, rubbish pit, broom.

Cover: pronunciation and vocabulary practice using this theme's words; a reading passage in this theme's context with comprehension questions; a grammar/language-use point suited to Grade 4; and a short piece of writing on this theme.
