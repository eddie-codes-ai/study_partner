Sub-strand 3.2 Light (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: identify the sources of light in nature; describe ways of lighting a house; explain the uses of light in day-to-day life; appreciate the applications of light in day-to-day life.

Content covered: sources of light; ways of lighting a house (windows, translucent roofs, artificial lighting); uses of light in day-to-day life (to see, safety, discourage pests, read comfortably).

Key inquiry question: Why is light important in day-to-day life?
