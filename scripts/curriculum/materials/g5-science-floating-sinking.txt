Sub-strand 3.1 Floating and Sinking (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: classify objects as floating or sinking in water; identify factors that affect floating and sinking of objects in water; explain applications of floating and sinking in day-to-day life; appreciate the use of floaters as life savers.

Content covered: floating and sinking in water; factors that affect floating and sinking of objects in water (shape, weight and size); applications of floating and sinking (swimming, diving, use of lifesavers, water transport, floods, drowning, surfing).

Key inquiry questions: Why do some materials float and others sink? How are floaters useful in day-to-day life?
