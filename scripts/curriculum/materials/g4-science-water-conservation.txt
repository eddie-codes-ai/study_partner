Sub-strand 2.3 Water conservation (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: explain the meaning of water conservation in the environment; describe methods of conserving water at home and school; outline the importance of conserving water at home and school; develop interest in conserving water at home and school.

Content covered: meaning of water conservation; methods of conserving water (simple examples of reducing water wastage, reusing water, and recycling water); importance of water conservation.

Key inquiry question: Why is it important to conserve water?
