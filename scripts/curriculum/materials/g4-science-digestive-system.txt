Sub-strand 1.3 Human digestive system (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify parts of the human digestive system; develop a plan of maintaining a healthy human digestive system; appreciate the importance of a healthy human digestive system.

Content covered: parts of the human digestive system (mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestines, large intestines, rectum, anus); a healthy digestive system (dental hygiene, deworming, healthy eating); symptoms of an unhealthy digestive system (stomach ache/pain, bloating, worms, diarrhoea, vomiting, constipation).

Key inquiry questions: What makes up the digestive system? How is a healthy digestive system maintained?
