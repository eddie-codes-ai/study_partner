Sub-strand 3.3 Slopes as simple machines (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: identify types of slopes used as simple machines; demonstrate how a slope makes work easier in day-to-day life; appreciate the use of slopes in everyday life.

Content covered: types of slopes (inclined planes); uses of slopes in everyday life to make work easier — ladders, ramps, staircases, roads winding up a hill, wedges, roofs, loading a lorry, elevators/lifts, escalators.

Key inquiry question: How are slopes used in everyday life?
