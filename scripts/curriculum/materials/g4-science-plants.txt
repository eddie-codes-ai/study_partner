Sub-strand 1.1 Plants (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify characteristics of plants as living things; describe functions of external parts of plants; appreciate the need to care for plants.

Content covered: characteristics of plants as living things (plants grow, reproduce, remove waste, respond to changes in their environment, and die); functions of external parts of plants (roots, stems and leaves).

Key inquiry question: Why are plants grouped as living things?
