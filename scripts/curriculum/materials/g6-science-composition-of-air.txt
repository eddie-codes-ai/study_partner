Sub-strand 2.2 Composition of Air (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: identify the components of air; outline uses of the different components of air; explain the effects of air pollution in the environment; describe methods of reducing air pollution in the environment; appreciate the need for clean air in day-to-day life.

Content covered: composition of air in the atmosphere; uses of different components of air; air pollution and its effects on the environment.

Key inquiry question: How does air pollution affect the environment?
