Sub-strand 1.2 Vertebrates (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Note: KICD's own summary table labels this sub-strand "Invertebrates," but the actual lesson content in the curriculum design body is entirely about Vertebrates (general characteristics, and classifying them into mammals, birds, reptiles, fish and amphibians) — that mismatch appears to be a typo in KICD's summary table, not the lesson content, so "Vertebrates" is used here as the accurate topic name.

Specific learning outcomes: describe general characteristics of vertebrates; classify vertebrates into their main groups; appreciate the importance of vertebrates in the environment.

Content covered: general characteristics of vertebrates; groups of vertebrates — mammals, birds, reptiles, fish and amphibians (structural features only).

Key inquiry question: What are the key features of vertebrates?
