Sub-strand 3.3 Heat (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: identify sources of heat in nature; demonstrate uses of heat in day-to-day life; describe safety measures to observe when using heat; appreciate the importance of heat in day-to-day life.

Content covered: sources of heat (sun, fire, electricity, gas); uses of heat (cooking, warming, ironing, drying, food preservation); safety measures when handling heat (use of kitchen gloves, avoid direct contact with open flames, use of appropriate clothing).

Key inquiry questions: What is the importance of heat? What are the safety measures observed when using heat?
