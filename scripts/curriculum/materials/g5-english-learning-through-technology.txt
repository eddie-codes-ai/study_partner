Theme "Learning Through Technology" (KICD Grade 5 English curriculum design). Like every English theme in this curriculum, it's taught across all four strands together — Listening & Speaking, Reading, Language Use (grammar), and Writing — using this theme's vocabulary and context throughout.

Suggested vocabulary: download, upload, delete, attach, create, website, wireless, online, laptop, tablet, desktop, research, source.

Cover: pronunciation and vocabulary practice using this theme's words; a reading passage in this theme's context with comprehension questions; a grammar/language-use point suited to Grade 5; and a short piece of writing on this theme.
