Sub-strand 1.2 Animals (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify characteristics of animals as living things; distinguish vertebrates from invertebrates in the environment; appreciate the need to care for animals.

Content covered: characteristics of animals as living things (animals feed, grow, breathe, reproduce, remove waste, move, respond to changes in their environment, and die); vertebrates and invertebrates — the main differences between them, and identifying examples of each in their locality.

Key inquiry question: Why are animals grouped as living things?
