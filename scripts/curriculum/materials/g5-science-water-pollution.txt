Sub-strand 2.2 Water Pollution (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: identify water pollutants in water sources; outline the effects of water pollution in day-to-day life; identify methods of reducing water pollution in water sources; apply appropriate methods of water treatment; advocate for safe water sources in the locality.

Content covered: meaning of the term water pollution; common water pollutants; effects of polluted water on living things; methods of reducing water pollution; basic methods of water treatment (boiling, filtration, chemical treatment, solar treatment).

Key inquiry question: What are the dangers of water pollution?
