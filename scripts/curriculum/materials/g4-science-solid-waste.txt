Sub-strand 2.2 Management of solid waste (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: classify solid waste into decomposable and non-decomposable; describe the dangers of solid waste to the environment; apply appropriate methods to manage solid waste in the environment; appreciate the need for proper management of solid waste.

Content covered: types of solid waste — decomposing (e.g. kitchen waste) and non-decomposing (plastic, metals, food wrappers); dangers of solid waste to the environment; managing waste in places (classrooms, schools, home, public functions); methods of managing solid waste — reduce, reuse and recycle; safety measures during solid waste management (protective gear, appropriate tools).

Key inquiry question: How is solid waste dangerous to the environment?
