Mada "Dira" (Direction/Compass) — KICD Grade 4 Kiswahili curriculum design. Kama kila mada katika mtaala huu, inafunzwa katika ujuzi wote wanne pamoja: Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza, Kusoma, Matumizi ya Lugha (sarufi), na Kuandika — yote yakihusiana na muktadha wa mada hii.

Like every Kiswahili theme in this curriculum, "Dira" (Direction/Compass) is taught across all four strands together — Listening & Speaking, Reading, Language Use (grammar), and Writing — using this theme's context throughout. Cover: pronunciation/vocabulary practice in this theme's context; a reading passage with comprehension questions; a grammar/language-use point suited to Grade 4; and a short piece of writing on this theme. All content should be in Kiswahili, matching this subject's existing content in the app.
