Sub-strand 3.2 Sound Energy (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: identify sources of sound in nature; demonstrate the movement of sound in nature; describe effects of loud sound in day-to-day life; appreciate the role of sound in day-to-day life.

Content covered: sources of sound (vibrating air, vibrating strings, vibrating drums); movement of sound in nature (sound travels in all directions from a source, including around corners); reflection of sound / echo; effects of loud sound; role of the government in addressing sound pollution.

Key inquiry questions: How is sound produced? What are the effects of loud sound?
