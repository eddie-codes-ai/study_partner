Sub-strand 1.1 Fungi (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify common fungi in the environment; describe the importance of fungi in nature; appreciate the importance of fungi in the economy.

Content covered: common fungi (mushrooms, toadstool, puff balls, yeast and moulds); importance of fungi (food, fermentation, health and medicine).

Key inquiry question: What is the importance of Fungi?
