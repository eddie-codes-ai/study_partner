Strand 3.0 Force and Energy (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design). This strand has three sub-strands — cover all three:

1. Floating and Sinking: classify objects as floating or sinking in water; factors that affect floating and sinking (shape, weight, size); applications in day-to-day life (swimming, diving, lifesavers, water transport, floods, surfing).

2. Sound Energy: sources of sound (vibrating air, strings, drums); sound travels in all directions from its source, including around corners; reflection of sound / echo; effects of loud sound; the government's role in addressing sound pollution.

3. Heat transfer: modes of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation); classifying conductors of heat as good or poor; uses of heat transfer in day-to-day life (cooking, melting, freezing, maintaining body temperature, insulation); safety precautions when handling heat and responding to fire emergencies.

Key inquiry questions: Why do some materials float and others sink? How is sound produced? What are the effects of loud sound? How is heat transferred through materials in nature?
