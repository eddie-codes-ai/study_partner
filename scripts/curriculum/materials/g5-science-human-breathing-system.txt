Sub-strand 1.3 The Human Breathing System (KICD Grade 5 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 1.0 Living Things and their Environment).

Specific learning outcomes: identify the main parts of the human breathing system; describe the functions of parts of the human breathing system; outline the symptoms and prevention measures for common conditions and diseases of the breathing system; appreciate the need for maintaining a healthy breathing system.

Content covered: parts of the breathing system and their functions (nose, trachea, lungs, diaphragm); symptoms and prevention of common conditions and diseases of the breathing system (common colds, coughs, COVID-19, allergy and asthma).

Key inquiry questions: What makes up the human breathing system? What enhances a healthy breathing system?
