Sub-strand 2.1 Properties of matter (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: categorise substances in the environment into the three states of matter; describe the properties of the three states of matter; appreciate the importance of the different states of matter in day-to-day life.

Content covered: meaning of matter; states of matter (solid, liquid and gas); properties of matter (shape, volume and mass) for each of the three states; importance of the different states of matter in day-to-day life.

Key inquiry question: What are the properties of matter?
