Sub-strand 2.1 Change of state (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 2.0 Matter).

Specific learning outcomes: identify the changes of state when substances are heated or cooled; describe the applications of the change of state of matter in everyday life; appreciate the applications of change of state in day-to-day life.

Content covered: changes of state of matter — melting, evaporation, sublimation, deposition, condensation and freezing; applications of change of state of matter in everyday life.

Key inquiry question: How is change of state of matter important in day-to-day life?
