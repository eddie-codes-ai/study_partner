Sub-strand 3.1 Force and its effect (KICD Grade 4 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: describe types of forces in nature; demonstrate the effects of force on objects in nature; describe the uses of force in daily life; appreciate the importance of force in day-to-day life.

Content covered: types of forces (force of gravity and force of friction); effects of force on objects (change of shape, start motion, stop moving objects, increase speed, decrease speed, change direction, hold objects together); uses of force in day-to-day life (wheelbarrow, tug of war, pulling and pushing a hand-cart, ox cart, pushing a bicycle, falling bodies); ways of minimising friction between moving bodies (smoothening surfaces, greasing, oiling, use of rollers, ball bearings).

Key inquiry question: How does force affect objects?
