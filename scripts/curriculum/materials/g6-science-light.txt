Sub-strand 3.1 Light (KICD Grade 6 Science & Technology curriculum design, Strand 3.0 Force and Energy).

Specific learning outcomes: demonstrate the movement of light through materials; draw ray diagrams of images formed on plane mirrors; illustrate the formation of shadows and eclipses in nature; describe the formation of rainbow in nature; appreciate the importance of movement of light in everyday life.

Content covered: movement of light through materials (transparent, translucent and opaque); ray diagrams of images in plane mirrors; formation of shadows and eclipses (solar & lunar); reflection of light at plane surfaces; image formation in plane mirrors; rainbow formation; devices that use light (mirrors, periscope, kaleidoscope, lenses, magnifying glass, hand lens, mirage).

Key inquiry question: How does light travel?
